How to read kappa (Landis & Koch, 1977)

KappaAgreement
below 0Poor
0.00 to 0.20Slight
0.21 to 0.40Fair
0.41 to 0.60Moderate
0.61 to 0.80Substantial
0.81 to 1.00Almost perfect

These bands are a rough convention, not a test. Kappa can be low even when raw agreement is high if one label dominates.
